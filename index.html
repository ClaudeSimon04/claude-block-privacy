Claude Block Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Claude Block is a Chrome extension that blocks advertising, tracking requests, and unwanted popup pages. It is maintained independently as a modified version of uBlock Origin Lite.

Information used on your device

Claude Block processes website addresses, network-request addresses, page elements and content, and link interactions to decide which requests, elements, and popup windows to block. Its filtering scripts can inspect or modify parts of a page, page script behavior, and site storage when a filter requires this to prevent advertising or tracking. The popup guard briefly keeps the destination and timing of a link interaction in page memory to distinguish the chosen link from an extra page opened alongside it.

This processing takes place in your browser. Claude Block does not upload browsing history, page content, or link activity to the maintainer, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or telemetry service. It does not create an account for you or ask for payment information.

Information saved locally

The extension stores filtering preferences, site exceptions, selected filter lists, user-created filters, imported-list addresses, downloaded list data, compiled filtering rules, and related update metadata in Chrome's extension storage. Site exceptions and custom rules may identify websites you use. Limited diagnostic information can be kept in browser-session storage to help troubleshoot problems.

Claude Block does not use Chrome's sync-storage API to send these settings to a cloud account. Settings backups you choose to export are saved as files on your device.

Optional imported filter lists

The built-in filters are included in the extension package. If you add and enable a filter list by URL, Claude Block contacts that list's host to download and periodically refresh the list. A list may reference additional list files. Those hosts can receive the requested URL and normal connection information, including your IP address. Claude Block does not add your browsing history or the content of the page you are viewing to these requests.

Imported lists and custom filters are optional. Some advanced filtering instructions use Chrome's User Scripts feature when you choose to enable it. Only import lists you trust.

Reports and support

The report page prepares diagnostic information for you to review before sharing. It does not submit a report automatically. Diagnostic text may include extension and browser versions, operating system, enabled filters, site settings, filter-list addresses, and recent errors. Remove private information before sharing it.

If you email support, the maintainer receives your email address and the information you include. This information is used to respond and investigate the reported issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, private access tokens, or unrelated personal information.

Use, sharing, and retention

Information is used only for Claude Block's disclosed filtering, settings, and support features. It is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or used for creditworthiness or lending decisions. The maintainer does not receive locally processed browsing data unless you choose to include it in a support message.

Local preferences remain until you change or remove them, reset the settings, or uninstall the extension. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to respond, investigate the issue, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the address below.

Your controls

You can change filtering for a site, disable popup blocking, remove custom filters or imported lists, restrict site access through Chrome, or disable or uninstall Claude Block. Uninstalling removes the extension's Chrome storage, but exported backups and messages you sent separately are not controlled by the extension.

Contact

For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, email dopestuffsllc@gmail.com.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated when Claude Block's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
