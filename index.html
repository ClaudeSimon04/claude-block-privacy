Claude Block Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · Claude Block 0.3.0

Claude Block is a Chrome extension that blocks advertising, tracking requests, and unwanted popup pages. It is maintained independently as a modified version of uBlock Origin Lite.

Information used on your device

Claude Block processes website addresses, network-request addresses, page elements and content, and link interactions to decide which requests, elements, and popup windows to block. Its filtering scripts can inspect or modify parts of a page, page script behavior, and site storage when a filter requires this to prevent advertising or tracking. The popup guard briefly keeps the destination and timing of a link interaction in page memory to distinguish the chosen link from an extra page opened alongside it.

This processing takes place in your browser. Claude Block does not upload browsing history, page content, or link activity to the maintainer, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or telemetry service. It does not create an account for you or ask for payment information.

Information saved locally

The extension stores filtering preferences, site exceptions, selected bundled filter lists, user-created element filters, local developer rules, and related settings in Chrome extension storage. Site exceptions and custom rules may identify websites you use. Limited diagnostic information, such as filtering errors, can be kept in browser-session storage to help troubleshoot problems.

Claude Block does not use Chrome's sync-storage API to upload these settings. Settings backups you choose to export are saved as files on your device and may include site exceptions and custom filters. Browser, device, and backup services you use may separately back up your profile or exported files.

Bundled filters and external links

All runtime code and filter instructions are included in the extension package. Updates to bundled filters arrive with extension releases through the distribution channel you use, such as the Chrome Web Store. Version 0.3.0 does not download filter lists or execute remotely hosted code, and it removes downloaded filtering data left by earlier versions.

If you open an external help, source-code, credit, or filter-provider link, your browser connects to that website under its own privacy practices. Google separately handles Chrome Web Store installation, updates, listings, reviews, and support data under Google's Privacy Policy.

Reports and support

The help/report page prepares diagnostic information locally for you to review and copy. It does not submit a report automatically. Diagnostic text may include extension and browser versions, operating system, enabled bundled filters, site settings, and recent errors. Review and remove private information before sharing it.

If you email support, the maintainer receives your email address and the information you include. This information is used to respond and investigate the reported issue. Email is handled by the mail providers involved, including Gmail for the support address below. Do not send passwords, payment details, private access tokens, or unrelated personal information. Public reviews and support posts may be visible to others.

Use, sharing, and retention

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information is used only for Claude Block's disclosed filtering, settings, and support features. It is not sold, used for personalized advertising, or used for creditworthiness or lending decisions. The maintainer cannot read locally processed browsing data unless you choose to include it in a support message.

Support information is not shared for advertising. It may be handled by the support service you choose, used as necessary to resolve your request, or disclosed when legally required. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to respond, investigate the issue, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the address below.

Local preferences remain until you change or remove them, reset the relevant settings, or uninstall the extension. Browser-session data is temporary; link-intent data is short-lived page memory. Uninstalling removes the extension's Chrome storage. Exported backups, copies you shared, and support correspondence are separate and must be deleted separately.

Your controls

You can change filtering for a site, disable popup blocking, remove custom filters, restrict site access through Chrome, or disable or uninstall Claude Block. Some restrictions can reduce blocking. Incognito access is off unless you enable it in Chrome; if enabled, the same local filtering practices apply there.

Contact

For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, email dopestuffsllc@gmail.com.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated when Claude Block's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
